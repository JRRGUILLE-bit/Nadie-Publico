Synopsis

Gonzalo, María and Luis are in their twenties, share an apartment in Montevideo, and over the years have become a family without admitting it. It lets them love each other a lot and treat each other pretty badly.

Luis is about to land his first job as a lawyer when he plugs in an old coffee maker and the law firm catches fire. He helps evacuate the office and gets his boss out. Everyone else sees him as a hero; he is convinced he has just burned down his future by plugging in a coffee maker.

Gonzalo steals the coffee maker from the wreckage and discovers that someone tampered with it. Meanwhile, María follows a neighbor’s story about her missing daughter and a gold robbery during the dictatorship. Soon they discover that the two mysteries are far more connected than they imagined.

Team

Maite Piñeyrúa Segura

Co-creator · co-writer · director

Maite Piñeyrúa Segura is a Uruguayan director, screenwriter and photographer, with a degree in Audiovisual Communication from UCU. She directed Tumbero, awarded at FIEC, Dodecá and La Floresta, and wrote and directed Una vez casi hice un gol. She is co-creator, co-writer and director of Nadie te dijo que iba a ser así. She was selected for Málaga Talent 2025.

Guillermo Barbeito Rodríguez

Co-creator · co-writer · creative development

Guillermo Barbeito Rodríguez is co-creator and co-writer of Nadie te dijo que iba a ser así. His work on the series includes building the narrative world, shaping the season structure and developing the tone that brings together black comedy, political thriller and everyday absurdity.

Guillermo and Maite write the series together and share its authorship.

Malena Benavides

Executive producer

Executive producer. She coordinates development, production organization and relationships with partners, institutions and professional forums.

She also produced the documentary Tumbero. On this project, she sets priorities and maintains the practical conditions needed to move toward production.
