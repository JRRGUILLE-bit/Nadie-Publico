Project file / Project & team

The project

When a gold robbery tied to Uruguay's dictatorship crashes into their everyday lives, three young people in Montevideo's Old City are pulled into a political plot where every possible way out compromises them further.

Uruguayan fiction series, 6 episodes of 20–25 minutes. Black comedy and political thriller, created and written by Maite Piñeyrúa Segura and Guillermo Barbeito Rodríguez, directed by Maite Piñeyrúa Segura.

Project status

Active development

  • Pilot script, dossier, series bible and season arc developed.
  • Teaser completed; director attached: Maite Piñeyrúa Segura.
  • Executive production by Malena Benavides and Uruguayan coproduction with SKA Films / Ignacio “Nacho” Jaunsolo.

Maite Piñeyrúa Segura

Cocreator, cowriter and director of Nadie te dijo que iba a ser así. Her directing work shapes the series' tonal balance between intimacy, everyday absurdity and political tension.

She directed the documentary Tumbero, recognized and selected at national festivals, and was selected by ACAU for Campus Málaga Talent 2025 with this project.

Guillermo Barbeito Rodríguez

Cocreator and cowriter. He works on narrative architecture, world building and the articulation between black comedy, shared domestic life and political thriller.

As part of the creative team, he developed the project through DETOUR Series Lab and the subsequent writing, dossier, series bible, season arc and teaser stages.

Ignacio “Nacho” Jaunsolo / SKA Films

Executive coproducer, founder and CEO of SKA Films. His work spans production, directing, cinematography and audiovisual content development.

SKA Films is the project's Uruguayan coproduction partner, bringing industrial experience to development, financing and circulation.

Malena Benavides

Executive producer. She coordinates development, production organization and relationships with partners, institutions and industry spaces.

She also produced the documentary Tumbero. On this project, she structures priorities and the concrete conditions required to move toward production.
